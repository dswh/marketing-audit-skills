# Business audit

Understand the business before selecting marketing tactics.
Capture the customer's problem, the offer, who buys, how the business earns revenue, the price or revenue model, proof, current channels and buying path.
Name the business's stated growth problem separately from any measured marketing issue. Request evidence for causal claims.
Define the primary customer action and how it is counted. Add a quality or downstream-value measure, baseline, agreed target, available budget, team time and constraints. Unknown values stay To confirm.
For a registration business, distinguish form starts, completed valid registrations, attendance and payment. For ecommerce, distinguish orders, margin and repeat purchases. For software, distinguish demos, activation and paid accounts. For local services, distinguish enquiries and completed bookings.
Keep the scope to one offer. End with at most three unanswered questions that could change the goal or scope.
When useful, retain an original screenshot of the offer and primary CTA with its URL, viewport, date and visible state. Pass it to marketing-journey-audit for annotation; reuse captures instead of repeating them. Keep private account or customer details out of shareable evidence.

## Outputs

In goal mode, create Marketing-Goal.html and update section marketing-goal.
In context mode, create Business-Context.html and update section business-context. Save the same shared facts to .agents/marketing-business-context.md.
With no mode specified, create both briefs and the shared context.

## Working rules

Use the materials the user supplies. Read .agents/marketing-business-context.md and the actual existing reports when available. A filename alone is not the report contents.
Ask once for missing information that could change the result. The user may proceed with unknowns.
Research permitted public pages when browsing is available. Record the URL and date checked. A supplied URL is not evidence that it was opened. If access fails, ask for page text or record a research task.
Keep observed facts, customer statements, provider claims, assumptions and proposals distinct. Preserve product labels and sample limits. Do not invent metrics, customer quotes, people, budgets, sources or performance.
Use plain words, short bullets, a useful diagram or compact comparison, and a small source list. No decorative scores or unsupported earnings claims.
Create local draft files only. Sharing, publishing, account access, messages and spending require their own authorization.

## Report continuity

Each output must work as a standalone report. Add or update its matching section in Marketing-Strategy-and-Action-Plan.html while preserving all other sections and sources. Start the master if absent. Missing sections remain missing, not invented.
If files cannot be written, return the report content and intended filename. Mark owners, targets, budgets and copy as proposed until agreed.
