# Marketing audit blueprint

Use this when assembling a founder-facing audit or action plan.
Adapt the visuals to the business. Do not force an event funnel onto another business model.

## Main reading view

Start with the decision due now: what to approve, what to hold, money at risk, proposed accountable owner and evidence needed for the next release.
Give each section one job and one useful visual. Prefer a labeled flow, actual-versus-model comparison, annotated screenshot, decision branch or release gate over paragraphs.
Keep facts, assumptions, proposals and unknowns visibly distinct using words, not color alone.
Use a compact primary blueprint and link detailed working reports, source records and original captures. Preserve traceability without repeating the same budget and caveats in every section.
Use assets/blueprint.css when useful. Cards fit their contents. Keep primary text readable, diagrams labeled and layouts usable on mobile and in print. Do not use decorative scores, eyebrow labels, fixed empty boxes or unsupported red/green funnel stages.
For supplied numeric scenarios, use scripts/audit_economics.py when helpful. It calculates contribution needed, break-even buyers and acquisition costs with decimal arithmetic. It does not decide statistical power or turn assumptions into actuals.

## Decision rules

- A visible mismatch supports a specific finding, not a proven conversion loss. Show the affected handoff and check that it belongs to the target traffic.
- Missing data can justify diagnosis. It does not justify choosing ad creative, a channel or a large pilot.
- Put actuals beside models. Unknown actuals remain Unknown, not zero. Record the offer, cohort, source, window and counting rules.
- Reconcile unique buyers and money to finance. Show unmatched orders, non-attendee purchases, duplicate payments, refunds and incomplete windows separately.
- Define contribution before campaign costs: revenue net of relevant discounts, tax, refunds and variable fulfillment costs. Exclude costs already included in the campaign to avoid double counting. State the basis and whether estimates or actuals were used.
- Full planned campaign cost divided by modeled buyers is the minimum contribution per buyer needed to cover that envelope. It is not a finance-approved media cost ceiling.
- Ask finance for the contribution available for acquisition after required profit. Distinguish total campaign affordability from incremental cost and contribution for the next release. Gross order value is neither profit nor collected cash.
- For acquisition tests, changing who visits can change the denominator. At comparable spend, consider mature buyer volume, cost and contribution. Use visit conversion diagnostically. Use randomized assignment and an appropriate analysis population where feasible. A creative comparison does not establish ad incrementality.
- Size pilots around baseline, commercially useful difference, precision, observation window and capacity. A round budget or a computed number of buyers does not establish sufficient power.
- Every action has an owner, evidence, a cost cap or cost status, and a continue/revise/hold condition. Proposed roles remain proposed until assigned.

## Finish check

Can the founder see what happens next without reading the appendix?
Can every recommendation be traced to evidence or an explicit question?
Could changing one missing financial input reverse the recommendation?
Have you made that input prominent?
